FAQ

Common questions

What does a notary actually do?

A notary public is a state-appointed official who checks the identity of signers, confirms that they're signing willingly, and records the act in a journal. This helps prevent fraud.

What do I need to bring?

A valid, unexpired government photo ID for each signer, plus your unsigned documents. If your document needs witnesses, bring them too.

Can you notarize any document?

Most documents, yes, as long as they include proper notarial wording and the signer is present with valid ID. We can't notarize a document that's incomplete or that the notary has a personal interest in.

Can you help me fill out my document or tell me which form to use?

No. Notaries can't give legal advice or prepare documents. Please talk to an attorney or the agency that requested the document.

How much does it cost?

The notary fee is $10 for the first signature and $1 for each additional signature, plus a $40 travel fee. Locations outside our service area have an additional long-distance fee. Weekday appointments from 5:00 pm to 8:00 pm have an additional after-hours fee. You'll get your total before we head out.

Do you have an office?

No. We're fully mobile, so every appointment happens at a location that works for you.
